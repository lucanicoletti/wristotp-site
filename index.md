WristOTP

Two-factor codes on your Garmin watch. Works with Google Authenticator.

Free and open source. **[Get the code on GitHub](https://github.com/lucanicoletti/wristotp)**

WristOTP isn't in any app store and there are no downloads. You build the watch app and the phone app (Android or iPhone) from the code and install them yourself. The [README](https://github.com/lucanicoletti/wristotp#watch) shows how.

Export your accounts from Google Authenticator, scan the QR code with the phone app and send them to your watch. Codes are generated offline, on your watch.
