# WristOTP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

WristOTP is made of a phone app (Android and iOS) and a watch app for Garmin Connect IQ devices.

## What I collect

Nothing. There are no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting in WristOTP. I don't get to see any of your data.

## What the apps do on your devices

2FA account secrets you import from QR codes. They're kept on your phone (only while the app is open) and stored on your watch.

Camera (used only while you scan a QR code). No images are saved.

Photos (only the one you pick in the system photo picker). It's read to find QR codes. The image isn't copied or saved.

Bluetooth (used through the Garmin Connect app to send your accounts to your watch). Your accounts travel from your phone to your watch only through Garmin Connect.

## Saving and security

Phone app. Your accounts aren't saved. They're kept in memory only while the app is open, and they're gone when you close the app or tap Clear. It does save the PIN setting and the last sync (its time, the number of accounts and the watch name).

Watch. Your accounts are saved in the app's private storage. They aren't encrypted. The PIN is on by default. (You can switch it off in the phone app.) Only a salted hash of the PIN is saved. The PIN is a screen lock. It stops people using the app. It doesn't encrypt your accounts. From the 5th wrong attempt your watch makes you wait (30 s, doubling each time, up to 8 min). After 10 wrong attempts it wipes all accounts.

Uninstalling an app deletes all its saved data.

## Third parties

When the phone app talks to your watch, it goes through Garmin's Connect IQ software on your phone (the Garmin Connect app), which is covered by Garmin's own privacy policy. (The phone app includes Garmin's Connect IQ Mobile SDK only to talk to Garmin Connect. It doesn't send data anywhere else.) Garmin doesn't collect any data on behalf of WristOTP. No data is sent to any other party.

## Children

WristOTP isn't intended for children. No data is collected from anyone.
