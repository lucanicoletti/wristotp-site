# WristOTP Support

Email **luca.nicolett@gmail.com**. Please include your phone model, your watch model and which watch edition you have (WristOTP or WristOTP Free).

## Common questions

**The phone app says no watch is connected.**
Open the Watch tab and tap Choose, then pick your watch in Garmin Connect. Make sure Garmin Connect shows the watch as connected.

**The phone app says WristOTP is missing on the watch.**
Install WristOTP or WristOTP Free from the Connect IQ Store, then open it once on the watch.

**How many accounts can the watch hold?**
WristOTP Free holds one account. WristOTP holds up to 50.

**Some accounts are listed as not supported.**
WristOTP supports time-based codes (TOTP) with SHA-1 and SHA-256. SHA-512 and counter-based (HOTP) accounts can't be shown on the watch.

**I forgot my watch PIN.**
After 10 wrong tries the watch erases its accounts. Send them again from the phone app and choose a new PIN.

**Refunds for WristOTP on the Connect IQ Store**
Refunds follow the Connect IQ Store policy (within 48 hours of purchase). Try WristOTP Free first.
