# WristOTP Support

Email me at [luca.nicolett@gmail.com](mailto:luca.nicolett@gmail.com). Tell me your phone model, your watch model and which watch app you have (WristOTP or WristOTP Free).

### Common questions

**The phone app says no watch is connected.**
Open Garmin Connect and check your watch is connected there. On iPhone, tap Choose in the watch card and pick your watch. On Android the phone app uses the watch paired in Garmin Connect.

**The phone app says WristOTP is missing on the watch.**
Install WristOTP or WristOTP Free from the Connect IQ Store, then open it once on your watch.

**How many accounts fit on the watch?**
WristOTP Free: one account. WristOTP: up to 50.

**Which accounts are sent?**
The ones at the top of the list. (Drag them to change the order, or tap A–Z.) WristOTP Free gets only the first one. The glance shows the first one too (hidden while the PIN is on).

**Some accounts are "not supported".**
WristOTP supports time-based codes (TOTP) with SHA-1 and SHA-256. SHA-512 and counter-based (HOTP) accounts can't be shown on your watch.

**I forgot my watch PIN.**
After 10 failed attempts your watch wipes all accounts. Send them again from the phone app and choose a new PIN.

**Refunds (WristOTP on the Connect IQ Store).**
Refunds follow the Connect IQ Store policy (within 48 hours of purchase). Try WristOTP Free first.
